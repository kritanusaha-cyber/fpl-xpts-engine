Shadow Price

Fair price inverts the LP reduced cost into pounds: price + (xPts − λ·price − μpos − replacement) / λ. λ prices the budget and μ the positional quota, because FPL forces a 2/5/5/3 squad. Replacement is measured within role, not within FPL position — "MID" holds both wingers and holding midfielders, and ranking them together compares people doing different jobs. Roles are clustered from goal threat, creation and defensive workload.

Risk against reward

Rightward is more expected points over the horizon; upward is more volatile. Useful picks sit low and right. Captaincy wants the opposite corner.

Fair price against listed price

Above the diagonal is underpriced for the job the player does; below it, overpriced. Distance from the line is the disagreement, in pounds.
