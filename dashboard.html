Shadow Price

Value vs position is the LP reduced cost — xPts − λ·price − μpos. Both duals matter. λ prices the budget; μ prices the positional quota, because FPL forces a 2/5/5/3 squad, so you never choose a forward against the whole market — only against the other forwards you are compelled to field. Using λ alone made the metric structurally unfair between positions (76% of keepers positive against 1% of forwards, which said nothing about the players, since you replace a defender with a defender, never with a forward).
